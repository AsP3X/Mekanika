# Gadgets

## Goals
- Utility items that support industrial gameplay.

## Examples
- Atomic Disassembler (tiered mining tool).
- Free Runners (movement enhancement).
- Jetpack or similar.
- Configurator (already covered under Control System).

## Integration
- Energy consumption where applicable.
- Module compatibility with MekaSuit where relevant.
- Data-driven stats.

## Notes
