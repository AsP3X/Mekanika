# Industrial Turbine GUI

## Goals
- Display steam input, energy output, rotor status, and efficiency.
- Formation status and component summary.

## Layout
- Energy production gauge or rate.
- Steam consumption display.
- Rotor blade and coil summary.
- Efficiency indicator.
- Formation status.
- Port configuration access.

## Widgets
- Rate displays for steam and energy.
- Component counters.
- Status light for formed/running.
- Multiblock Data panel integration.

## Interaction
- Opens from any structural block.
- Configurator for ports.
- Clear feedback for imbalance or missing components.
