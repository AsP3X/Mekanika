# Individual Multiblock Specifications

## Dynamic Tank
- Flexible rectangular prism.
- Capacity based on interior volume.
- Ports for fluid/chemical input/output.
- Controller owns aggregated storage.

## Thermal Evaporation Plant
- Tower structure with solar panels.
- Brine → Lithium or other outputs.
- Height affects rate.
- Formation rules and valve placement.

## Thermoelectric Boiler
- Heat input → steam output.
- Superheating elements and pressure dispersers.
- Coolant requirements.
- Integration with turbines.

## Supercritical Phase Shifter (SPS)
- Polonium or other advanced chemical production.
- Specific internal components and ports.
- High energy and chemical requirements.

## Shared Rules
- All use the multiblock formation and invalidation framework.
- Mixed-tier internals where applicable.
- Debug tool reports formation status and component counts.
- Clear failure modes.
